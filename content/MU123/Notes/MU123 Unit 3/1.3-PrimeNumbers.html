1.3 Prime Numbers

  • You have seen that every natural number greater than 1 has at least two factors, itself and 1
  • A natural number that has exactly two factors, 1 and itself, is called a prime number
  • Examples of prime numbers are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, and 29
  • The prime numbers are building blocks of all natural numbers
  • Prime numbers play a central role in both abstract mathematics and practical applications such as cryptography
