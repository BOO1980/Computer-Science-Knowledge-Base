1.2 Factors

  • A natural number that divides into a second natural number is called a factor or divisor of the second number
  • ie. 2 is a factor of 10 since 2 divides exactly into 10
  • Factors are closely related to multiples, since "2 is a factor of 10" means the same "10 is a multiple of 2", anohter way of saying the same thing is "10 is divisible by 2"
  • Every natural number greater than 1 has at least two factors, itself and 1
  • But most number have more factors than this
  • eg. 12 has the factors 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12
  • The factors can be arranged into factor pairs, where the two factors in each pair multiplier together to give the number
  • eg. The factor pairs of 12 are (1, 12), (2, 6) and (3, 4)
  • Because the factors of a number form pairs, most numbers have an even number of factors
  • The only exceptions are the square numbers, which have an odd number of factors
  • Remember a square number is the result of multiplying a number by itself
  • eg. 36 is a square number since 6 x 6 = 36
  • The following tests are a useful way to determine if a number is a factor of another number
  • A number divisible by
    • 2 if it ends in 0,2,4,6 or 8
    • 3 if its digits add up to a multiple of 3
    • 5 if it ends in 0 or 5
    • 9 if its digits add up to a multiple of 9
  • Highest common factors can be useful when you are cancelling fractions
  • for example, when simplifying the fraction 24/30, you can divide both the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor, which is 6, to get 4/5

Common Factors

  • Common factors are factors that two or more numbers share
  • eg. The common factors of 12 and 18 are 1, 2, 3 and 6
  • The highest common factor (HCF) is the largest factor that two or more numbers share
  • eg. The HCF of 12 and 18 is 6
  • HCF is also known as the greatest common divisor (GCD)
